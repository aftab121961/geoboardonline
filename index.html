What is an Online Virtual Geoboard?

The virtual geoboard is an interactive digital math manipulative that enables students and educators to explore plane geometry concepts dynamically. Originally invented by mathematician Caleb Gattegno, a physical geoboard uses pegs and rubber bands to form geometric shapes. Our online geoboard brings this powerful geometry board to modern browsers with added tools such as protractors, rulers, freehand pen drawing, and multiple grid configurations.

Key Features of Our Geometry Tool for Students and Teachers

Versatile Grid Layouts

Switch between 5×5 and 10×10 square grids, dense isometric (triangular) grid, and circular ring geoboards to explore diverse geometric properties.

Elastic Rubber Bands

Stretch and snap colorful rubber bands across pegs to easily construct triangles, quadrilaterals, complex polygons, and circles.

Angle & Distance Tools

Measure precise angles using the built-in protractor tool and calculate side lengths with the virtual ruler overlay.

Freehand Pen & Presets

Annotate math problems, write notes directly on the board, and instantly load shape presets like unit squares and regular polygons.

Why Use a Digital Geometry Board in the Classroom?

As an essential geometry tool for students and teachers, the virtual geoboard simplifies teaching concepts such as perimeter, Pick's theorem, area of irregular shapes, coordinate geometry, angles, and symmetry. It allows teachers to present interactive math lessons on smartboards and enables students to experiment independently on computers or tablets without physical constraints.
